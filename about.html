About Union Basil Inc.

Union Basil Inc. is a Canadian pharmaceutical wholesaler and sourcing partner located in Welland, Ontario.

We specialize in sourcing and supplying pharmaceutical and healthcare products through reliable supply channels while maintaining a strong commitment to quality, compliance, and customer service.

Our Mission

To provide dependable pharmaceutical sourcing solutions by connecting healthcare organizations with quality products and trusted supply partners.

Our Commitment

We are committed to maintaining product integrity, regulatory compliance, and responsible pharmaceutical distribution practices.
